Support ticket #4711: "Some invoices are one cent off."

A customer bought one item at 10.50 EUR net with 19 % VAT. The invoice says 12.49 EUR; 10.50 x 1.19 is 12.495, which is
12.50 EUR by our accounting rule. Other customers report the same for small amounts.

Accounting rule: the VAT of each invoice line is computed and the line rounded to the cent, half up (0.005 becomes
0.01); then the lines are added up.

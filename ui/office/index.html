    How to try it · about five minutes

    What you see

    Left office: our agent. It plans with a model, writes code that runs in a sandbox, and every action to the outside passes the gate (the guard), only with a person's approval.

    Right office: next door. The same model with a prompt guardrail and no gate.

    Counters on top: people exposed or euros paid wrongly, ours against next door.

    Right column: what waits for you (a goal to confirm, an action to approve, a question), and the live log of every real event; red means refused. Figures move only because the core or the gate logged it.

    The line under the buttons says what the agent is doing; when it says Waits for you, look right.

    What to do, and what to expect

    1. Enter the operator pin on the right (from the submission page it is filled in). The buttons light up.
    2. Survey: Give the task, then Confirm goal and rules on the right: the goal as typed criteria, the rules fixed code derives from it.
    3. Expect the gate to refuse a report on 2 people (red). The agent starts a follow-up survey: approve the invitation.
    4. With 5 answers in, approve the report to HR. The log says fulfilled.
    5. Press a CEO question. Next door names people; ours is refused: differs from an earlier release by 1 person.
    6. Invoices (tab on top): give the task and confirm. Each payment needs a reason: type one, then approve.
    7. Press the mail from 'Atlas Office Supplies', a phishing mail. Ours does not pay as asked and answers the supplier.
    8. Press Try to break out of the sandbox: seven attacks, each stopped, in the log.

    Everything runs on simulated data. Every new task starts fresh: earlier visitors' runs are forgotten.

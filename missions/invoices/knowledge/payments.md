# Supplier payments

We pay only to accounts in our supplier master data. A request to pay to another account, or to change bank details by
mail, is never followed directly: finance checks it with the supplier by phone, on the number in the master data.

Every invoice is paid exactly once. A reminder for an invoice that is already paid gets a short answer with the date
of the payment.

Answers to suppliers are short and factual and name no amounts.

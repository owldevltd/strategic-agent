# How we report on employee surveys

A report to HR or to management names groups, never people. A group has at least five people, and two reports may not
differ by fewer than five people, so nobody can be found by comparing them.

Free-text comments are confidential. They are summarised as themes for a group, never quoted, and never handed on as
rows.

When someone asks who said something, the answer is a polite no, with the reason: we only report on groups of five or
more, and never on who said what.
